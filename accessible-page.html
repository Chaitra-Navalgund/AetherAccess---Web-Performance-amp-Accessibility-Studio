Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers regarding accessibility, payment, language interpretation, and booking.

All our community facilities offer zero-step ramp entrances, wide doorways conforming to ADA guidelines, adjustable height examination tables, trained American Sign Language (ASL) and certified medical interpreters for over 40 languages, sensory-calm rooms, and certified service animal relief areas.

No person is turned away regardless of insurance status or ability to pay. We accept Medicaid, Medicare, and commercial insurance. For uninsured neighbors, services are provided on a sliding fee scale based on household size, including 100% free community subsidized care.

You can select your accommodation preferences during online appointment booking, or call our 24/7 dedicated accessibility coordination line at (555) 019-2834 (TTY: 711). Free accessible medical transit vouchers are provided upon request.
